# Independent enquiry review sheet

During development, Bahaa and Mohamad act as the support and operations reviewers on the same synthetic or otherwise authorized cases. Use one copy per reviewer and enquiry. Each reviewer records their evidence and findings before seeing the other review, the benchmark answer, or the evaluator output. For company data, the pilot team confirms the applicable operating rules and verifies that the source records are suitable for review.

Both reviewers assess the same checks. The support reviewer pays particular attention to what the customer asked, what was communicated, and whether a correction addressed the request. The operations reviewer pays particular attention to booking and container identity, the records visible at send time, and the actual escalation, follow-up or closure. Use short source references and keep transcripts in the approved workspace.

A missed critical failure, unsupported resolution, or wrong customer or shipment link blocks the affected automated finding until the issue is reviewed and retested.
