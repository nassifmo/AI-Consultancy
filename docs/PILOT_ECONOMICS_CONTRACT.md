# Pilot economics: from service to money

This calculator consumes a **reconciled financial comparison**, not raw invoices or live agent replies. Use it after agreeing on the scope, reconciling the records and checking what the comparison supports. It runs locally and requires no API access.

```powershell
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m evaluator.pilot_economics pilot_prospect/ECONOMICS_EXAMPLE.json
```

The example has 100 eligible enquiries in each arm. The AI costs $0.80 more per enquiry for handling. The fictional downstream ledger shows $1.30 less dispatch expense per enquiry, so the observed scoped difference is $0.50 per enquiry. The example remains marked `illustrative_only`. The released time is 50 staff hours per 1,000 enquiries and is kept outside the financial total. Without using downstream costs, an extra verified timely and correct resolution would need to be worth $4.00 at the sample point estimate. The missing-outcome bounds give a conditional threshold from $3.20 to about $5.33. These numbers are invented to show the format.

| Improvement we might observe | What could turn it into money | Records to request and validate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A correct reply arrives before dispatch or a rescheduling cutoff | Fewer failed truck trips, missed pickups or rescheduling fees | Request ID and timestamps, shipment/container ID, status visible when the reply was sent, appointment cutoff, actual dispatch or cancellation, final invoice/credit, payer |
| A correct pickup or delay update helps recover time before free time expires | Lower terminal storage, demurrage or detention expense for the payer, where the reply could change the outcome | Release and availability history, free-time deadline and terms, gate or empty-return events, final charges and waivers, payer |
| Fewer repeat contacts or less rework | Released staff capacity; lower spending when paid overtime or outsourced work actually falls | Case linkage and active work logs, payroll or supplier invoice, comparable queue and demand, finance confirmation |
| Fewer incorrect answers or missed escalations | Less paid remediation or service credits, where the records establish the cause | Reply and action trail, affected incident, credit/compensation entry, chargebacks or refunds, payer |

Response speed alone is not a price. A faster but incorrect answer may increase losses. Measure a **timely, correct answer or action** before an agreed deadline and report speed and accuracy separately. Use a similar randomized/default comparator if possible, keep requests that failed or handed off in the denominator, and link charges using stable case and shipment IDs. Check whether a correct reply could have changed an action before the operational cutoff. Tariffs show possible charges. Settlement status, credits and a credible comparator are needed for claims about realized savings.

For example, a correct ETA at 10:00 instead of 12:00 could matter if the customer could still cancel a truck at 11:00. Check whether the cancellation happened, whether it changed the net charge, and whether the comparison shows that this happens more often with AI. A fee avoided by the shipper is not automatically money saved by the carrier. It could also be revenue forgone by the party that would have charged it. This calculator covers scoped expenses; use independently validated contribution margin, not gross sales, for any future retention or revenue claim, and report material revenue effects before making a broader profitability conclusion.

### Losses from wrong answers

A wrong pickup confirmation can send a truck to the terminal before the container is ready. A correction sent after dispatch may resolve the enquiry while the trip charge still stands. In the incident record, retain the original reply and what was known when it was sent, the correction and its time, the dispatch decision, and the related invoice or compensation after credits and recoveries. Record who bore the expense. If the truck was already committed or the charge would have arisen anyway, the original reply does not establish an incremental loss. Report customer expenses separately from carrier expenses. [Maersk charge and dispute examples](https://www.maersk.com/it-it/local-information/north-america/united-states-of-america/import)

Track how often costly incidents occur and how large their losses are. Review material incidents individually, even when the average service and financial metrics are favorable. If the financial evidence is missing, record the exposure separately from incurred costs and keep the amount unknown. The financial comparison uses the net expense difference between comparable policies, so ordinary costs that also occur under human handling are represented in the comparator. Credit averted harm separately from incurred loss and count each economic item once. [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/1-sec-risk/)

In **Upgrade value → Financial downside of wrong answers**, we can enter an additional loss for the selected policy per 1,000 comparable enquiries. It raises the scoped cost change by that amount divided by 1,000 and lowers the illustrative net value by the same amount when a value per success is supplied. Enter only the incremental loss outside those other amounts. One hypothetical $1,200 loss per 1,000 enquiries adds $1.20 per enquiry and turns a $1.00 apparent saving per enquiry into a $0.20 cost increase. If the scenario brings a range that otherwise supported a monitored expansion trial to zero or below, the recommendation calls for finance and operations to check the loss evidence first. The downloads retain the assumption. Use the reconciled downstream buckets for actual expense records.

### Inputs and interpretation

- Set the party whose economics are in scope, a common currency and a half-open study period. Another party’s expense requires a separate run. Wrapping both arms in one period does not prove they are comparable. `scope_ref` should identify the written eligibility, follow-up, channel, assignment and allocation rules; `comparable_scope_ref` records who checked that both arms implement the same definitions, include failures, and use the same denominators, cost horizon and accounting basis. The script shows documented subtotals without a unit comparison if this reference is missing.
- Arm counts represent all eligible enquiries with duplicates reconciled. Each outcome has `verified` and `unknown` counts. The rest are known non-events. Omit the entire outcome if coverage cannot be categorized. A reviewer must approve and record both the candidate’s critical failure limit and timely-and-correct floor before a financial review can advance. The gates use the worst case allowed by unknown outcomes. Define a defensible minimum study size and uncertainty for these gates before approving operational use.
- Buckets are the reviewed ledger scope, with separate evidence references proving completeness for each arm. Each line is unique, uses the stated payer, currency and date, and has a traceable ledger reference. Signed amounts may include credits and adjustments. Use decimal strings for financial amounts. The data preparer must check upstream that different line IDs are not duplicate economic items, and that shared costs are allocated once. A reconciled empty bucket means zero qualifying recorded expense. A missing bucket or reconciliation means incomplete evidence. Include handling costs for both arms on the same basis: vendor and usage charges, incremental operating expenses, setup allocated to the agreed horizon, oversight, exceptions and staffing costs that belong in the expense scope. Do not mix assumed dollar values of staff time with actual expenses.
- For downstream cost use `included` and reconciled buckets, with a `downstream_linkage_ref` pointing to the sample's case/shipment, decision cutoff, action and ledger crosswalk. The crosswalk shows the route by which a reply might change expense; the comparison still has to estimate the expense that changed. Use `not_assessed` to leave net expense unknown. Use `out_of_scope` only to publish a specifically **handling-only** result. Never add a faster-resolution monetary scenario to the same downstream benefit already in the ledger. Reconcile active work separately with a coverage reference; unchanged pay or headcount does not establish cash savings.
- If direct financial evidence is missing or incomplete, the break-even estimate still gives the cost per extra verified timely and correct resolution when handling cost is available. The threshold is not a willingness-to-pay estimate. When outcome missingness could erase the gain, the worst-case threshold is unbounded. Do not promote this result into a hit rate or a rollout rule.
- The CLI computes observed differences and checks whether supplied review evidence is present. It does not validate signed documents or estimate a causal interval. A decision package requires verified finance and attribution review references and a reviewed uncertainty interval for the **same** scoped net difference, along with service evidence. With a positive interval the output is only `supports_financial_review`. The final decision remains with the owner. Verified on-time outcomes and the handling cost difference are useful even when the monetary impact is unknown.
